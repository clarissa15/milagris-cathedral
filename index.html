A Place of Miracles
Since 1652

Milagris Cathedral — dedicated to Our Lady of Miracles — stands as one of the most beloved pilgrimage churches on the Konkan coast. For over 374 years, the faithful have gathered beneath Her gaze to seek intercession, celebrate sacraments, and find peace.

The cathedral's striking Gothic facade, adorned with the Alpha and Omega and crowned by a glowing red cross, gives way to a breathtaking interior — with a sky-painted octagonal dome, vivid stained glass, and an ornate gilded altarpiece bearing the INRI cross at its heart.
